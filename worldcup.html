How qualification & tie-breakers work

48 teams, 12 groups of four. The top two in each group go through automatically, and the 8 best third-placed teams across all groups fill the rest — 32 teams reach the Round of 32.

Tie-breakers within a group (2026 rules) — head-to-head now comes before overall goal difference, a change from 2022, and drawing of lots is gone:

  1. Points
  2. Head-to-head points (among the tied teams)
  3. Head-to-head goal difference
  4. Head-to-head goals scored
  5. Overall goal difference
  6. Overall goals scored
  7. Team conduct score (cards)
  8. FIFA World Ranking

Third-placed teams are in different groups, so no head-to-head: points → goal difference → goals scored → conduct → FIFA ranking. Top 8 advance.
