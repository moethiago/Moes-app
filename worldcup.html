Road to 32

Set the scores, see who's through. Top two in every group reach the Round of 32, plus the 8 best third-placed teams. Tap any team for its exact qualification path.

The format

48 teams, 12 groups of four (A–L). Every team plays the other three once. The top two in each group advance automatically — that's 24 teams — and the 8 best third-placed teams across all 12 groups fill the last spots, for 32 teams in the Round of 32.

Tie-breakers within a group (2026 rules)

When teams are level on points, FIFA now checks head-to-head before overall goal difference — a change from 2022. Drawing of lots is gone.

  1. Points
  2. Head-to-head points (among the tied teams)
  3. Head-to-head goal difference
  4. Head-to-head goals scored
  5. Overall goal difference
  6. Overall goals scored
  7. Team conduct score (cards)
  8. FIFA World Ranking

Ranking the third-placed teams

They're in different groups, so no head-to-head: points → goal difference → goals scored → conduct → FIFA ranking. The top 8 go through.
